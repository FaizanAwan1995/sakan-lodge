Privacy Policy

SAKAN Lodge · Last updated 9 November 2026

SAKAN Lodge operates shared accommodation in South Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information through our accommodation management website and internal administration application.

Information we collect

We may hold resident names, contact details, room and property assignments, tenancy dates, rent and bond records, payment information, maintenance requests, and related correspondence. We collect information provided by residents or recorded by authorised property managers for accommodation administration.

How we use information

We use information to manage accommodation, maintain financial records, respond to enquiries, and send relevant property or tenancy notices. The internal management application is intended for authorised SAKAN Lodge administrators, not resident logins.

Email communications and Google permissions

When configured, authorised administrators may use a shared SAKAN Lodge Gmail account to send messages to selected residents. The Google Gmail send permission is used to send authorised messages only; it does not grant the application permission to read, modify, or delete mailbox messages. We do not sell tenant email addresses. Bulk messages should use blind carbon copy (BCC) to avoid revealing recipient addresses to other residents.

Website enquiries and reservation alerts

Enquiry forms submit your contact details, preferred accommodation and message directly to our private database. New reservation requests and enquiries generate email alerts to the SAKAN Lodge mailbox and authorised administrators. We use Cloudflare Turnstile and a protected network identifier to limit spam. An enquiry or reservation request does not confirm a booking.

Online agreement signing

When we invite you to sign an agreement, we collect each named resident’s typed name, typed or drawn signature, electronic signing consent and submission time. We retain the original and signed agreement, document verification hashes, review decisions, browser information and a protected network identifier for tenancy administration and an audit record. Signing pages use private links with an expiry and do not include advertising or analytics. Anyone with the complete link can access that agreement until it expires or is revoked, so keep it private. Authorised administrators review signatures and applicant identity before approving room allocation. You can download your signed copy from the signing page while the link is valid, or contact us for a copy.

Optional advertising measurement

If Google Ads enquiry measurement is configured, the enquiry form offers an optional measurement choice. The Google tag loads only after you opt in. It measures a successfully received enquiry using a random submission reference; we do not send your form name, email, phone number or message as conversion fields. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and advertising click information. Enquiry submission works without opting in. See Google’s privacy policy.

AI room assistant

If you use the AI assistant, your question and up to six previous question-and-answer pairs are sent through our website to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate accommodation guidance. Please do not enter personal, financial or sensitive information. The assistant receives curated public property information and has no access to private resident records. Conversation history and room preferences are kept in this tab’s session storage for up to 24 hours so you can resume after a refresh. Start a new chat to clear this context; closing the tab ends the browser session. We do not save chat transcripts in our database. Email addresses and phone-like strings in chat are filtered before sending, but please keep personal information out of chat. If you complete the welcome form, your name, email and phone are saved as an accommodation enquiry for our team to contact you. Future availability updates require the separate optional consent checkbox; your choice and time are recorded with the enquiry. Contact fields are not included in AI prompts or browser chat storage. Cloudflare processes requests under its service policies. Network identifiers are used for request limits. Answers may be inaccurate; our team must confirm availability, prices and booking terms.

Resident reviews

When you submit a review, we collect your chosen display name, email, rating, comments, optional suburb and publishing consent. Your email remains private and is used to verify your stay. Approved reviews display your chosen name, rating, comments and selected suburb on our website. We use Cloudflare Turnstile and a protected network identifier to help prevent spam. Contact us to request a correction or removal of a published review.

Suburb maps

Our public suburb pages include Google Maps. When a map loads, your browser connects to Google to display it. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, under its privacy policy. Maps show suburb boundaries and administrator-approved property positions. Our property marker labels show house names rather than street addresses; an exact map pin nevertheless makes the location identifiable. Directions and nearby searches are displayed on our website using Google Maps Platform.

Storage and service providers

Our application uses hosting, authentication, and database services, which may include Cloudflare, Supabase, and Google. Data may be processed or stored in locations used by those providers, subject to their configurations and safeguards. Access should be limited to authorised administrators and service providers as necessary.

Security and retention

We apply access controls and take reasonable steps to protect information. We retain records while needed for tenancy administration, legal obligations, and dispute resolution, and then delete or de-identify them when appropriate. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Access, corrections and enquiries

Residents may contact us to ask about their information, request corrections, or raise a privacy concern. We will assess requests in light of applicable Australian law and recordkeeping obligations.
