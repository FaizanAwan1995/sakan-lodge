BEFORE YOU MOVE

Good to know

Can university students apply for a room?

Yes. Domestic and international university students can enquire about SAKAN Lodge rooms. Check the advertised room, stay length and route to your campus. These are independently managed rooms, rather than university-operated accommodation.

Do you offer rooms for working professionals?

Yes. Working professionals can enquire about furnished private rooms and shared accommodation in Fulham, Clapham and Oaklands Park. Choose a location that suits your workplace and check availability for your full stay.

Can couples enquire about accommodation?

Yes. Tell us there are two occupants. Our team must confirm room capacity, sleeping arrangements and availability. Rooms with capacity above one are shared accommodation and are priced per person; single-occupancy private rooms are for one person.

Are bills included, and what are the weekly rates?

Electricity, water, gas and internet are included without excess utility charges. Long-term shared rooms are $185 per person/week. Private rooms are $255/week long-term or $300/week short-term. A stay of exactly two calendar months qualifies as long-term; shared rooms are long-term only.

Are heating and air-conditioning provided?

Heaters are provided, and air-conditioners are available on request. Contact our team to arrange heating or cooling for your stay.

Can I bring my own appliances?

Electricity, water, gas and internet are included in your rent. Other appliances with high electricity consumption require prior approval from SAKAN Lodge administrators. Please ask our team before bringing them.

Do you offer women-only accommodation?

Yes, we are introducing a women-only household at Sakan Fulham. The transition is in progress; speak to our team about your dates and we will confirm when the household and your room are ready. Explore women’s accommodation.

Who can enquire, and what are the household arrangements?

Explore men-only household options, women-only accommodation being introduced at Sakan Fulham, and enquiries for couples. Our team confirms the household arrangement, move-in date, room capacity and availability before a stay is agreed.

Is SAKAN Lodge halal-suitable?

We offer a halal-suitable living environment. Please discuss your household preferences with our team so we can confirm a suitable placement for your stay.

Where in South Australia are your rooms?

Our advertised locations are Fulham, Clapham and Oaklands Park in Adelaide, South Australia. Explore the suburb pages for advertised rooms and local-area information. Check your campus or workplace journey from the actual property.
