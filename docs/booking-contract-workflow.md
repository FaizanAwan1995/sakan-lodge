# Simple contract and manual booking workflow

1. A public submission creates a pending request. Turnstile success verifies spam protection, not booking acceptance.
2. Admin reviews prefilled applicant, contact, dates, room, agreed rent, bond and verified private property address, then generates the contract PDF.
3. The branded A4 agreement follows the current CBS rooming-house agreement structure (September 2025), with SAKAN house rules and inclusive utilities. Standard source: https://www.cbs.sa.gov.au/documents/tenancy/forms/Rooming-house-agreement.pdf. No government endorsement is implied. Admin reviews all terms before generation.
4. The proprietor signature artwork, expressly authorised for reuse by the owner, is extracted from the supplied contract and stored only in staff-only private defaults. New snapshots freeze that artwork and their generation date. No resident signature or historical execution date is copied. Existing signed contracts and PDFs are immutable and unchanged.
5. Admin may review the generated PDF and click Send booking confirmation email. The exact recipient and message are previewed before sending from sakanlodges@gmail.com. The attachment is the reviewed stored PDF. The message makes clear that final acceptance follows admin verification of the signed agreement, bond and availability. It requests PayID 0426721787 and replies containing signed agreement and receipt. No email sends just because a PDF is generated.
6. Later, admin confirms the reservation manually, acknowledging their checks. No signed-PDF upload, payment receipt or bank details are required by the panel. The database locks the room, rechecks capacity and current agreement, assigns dated ledger residents and removes duplicate reservation allocation atomically. Shared rooms retain unallocated beds; fully allocated rooms leave the public catalog.
7. Manual confirmation does not invent a payment, mark a bond as paid, or record a resident signature. Bond is initially Not paid; staff record actual payments and retained documents separately. Existing verified-payment workflows remain available internally for historical compatibility but are not the main panel flow.

Original records, resident rates and issued agreements are preserved. No final email is sent automatically on manual confirmation. Actual check-in remains separate. Assigned-tenant cancellation/refund requires the existing ledger process. Email uncertain-send reconciliation and duplicate protection remain in effect.
