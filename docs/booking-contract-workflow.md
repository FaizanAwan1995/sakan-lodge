# Reviewed contract and booking workflow

1. A website submission is a pending request. Turnstile verifies bot protection; its success is not booking acceptance. The widget uses interaction-only appearance and is hidden after receipt.
2. Admin verifies prefilled applicant name, email, contact, arrival/departure, room, rent, bond and private premises. The supplied source terms and proprietor defaults are prefilled privately. Admin verification can approve the source template as part of Generate contract PDF; there is no required JSON upload or separate template-creation step.
3. A frozen contract version produces a real A4 PDF in private storage. Admin can review the PDF in the reservation panel or open/download it. Stored generated PDFs cannot be overwritten. Existing signatures are never reused.
4. Approve PDF and send provisional booking email previews the exact recipient/message and sends through the lodge Gmail account with the reviewed PDF attached. The notice requests the bond via PayID 0426721787, a signed contract reply and payment receipt. It explicitly says final confirmation follows payment/signature/availability verification and remaining rent may be paid on arrival. No email is sent merely by viewing or generating a contract.
5. Admin uploads and verifies all signatures, uploads private payment evidence, enters the actual received amount/date/bank reference and verifies the bank transaction. Final confirmation requires the exact signed bond and prevents stale or duplicate confirmation.
6. Final confirmation atomically creates dated ledger residents, records actual bond payments as liabilities, and removes the duplicate reservation allocation. Status is assigned, not physical check-in; future tenants appear as upcoming in Rooms. A fully allocated room is hidden from the catalog, while unallocated shared spaces remain. Actual arrival is recorded separately, within the agreed stay.

Existing residents, rates, historic contracts and payments are unchanged. Source clauses remain subject to admin review: obsolete electricity excess wording is replaced by all-inclusive utilities, and the sample’s immediate-eviction penalty is excluded from the supplied draft. Do not treat this as a legal-compliance certification. The complete original source remains unchanged and private.

Email claims, immutable recipient/body, current-contract checks and uncertain-send reconciliation prevent duplicate/replaced sends. The required sender is sakanlodges@gmail.com. No automated retry is made after an uncertain outcome. Cancellation/refund of an assigned paid tenant requires separate ledger/bond review; this workflow does not invent a refund policy or delete financial records.
